// Overview

ABOUT THE WATERMARK TOOL

The Image Watermark Tool adds custom text overlays to photos directly in your browser. Watermarking protects photographers, content creators, businesses, and anyone sharing images online from having their work used without credit or permission.

Image theft is widespread in India on social media and WhatsApp. Adding a watermark with your name, Instagram handle, or brand makes it hard for others to use your images without attribution. Even if someone screenshots your watermarked image, your name remains visible.

All processing happens locally — images are never uploaded to our servers, ensuring privacy for personal and business photos.

// Tips

WATERMARK TIPS

  • Place watermarks where they cannot easily be cropped out
  • 50-70% opacity balances visibility and aesthetics
  • Include your website URL or Instagram handle
  • For client previews use larger prominent watermarks, removed after payment
